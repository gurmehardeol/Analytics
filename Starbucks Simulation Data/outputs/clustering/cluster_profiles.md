## Cluster 1 — "Grab-and-Go Commuter" (60,600 orders · 60.6%)

### Persona Summary
Older, convenience-driven customers who walk in or drive through on weekdays. They order smaller, simpler drinks, spend less per visit, and are less likely to be rewards members. They represent the high-frequency, low-customization mainstream.
